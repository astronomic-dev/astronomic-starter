The site uses the default look, Minimal. A preset swaps it for another look on every page.

## Switch the preset

Open `src/styles/global.css` and uncomment one look line (remove its `/*` and `*/`), such as Soft or Ink. Save, and the dev server shows the new look right away.

## Light and dark

Every preset has a light mode and a dark mode. Visitors switch between them with the button in the navigation bar.
